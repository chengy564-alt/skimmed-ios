What Skimmed does

Upload a statement. See everything your bank never showed you.

No bank connection. No account. One statement in, and every hidden cost of your last trip is laid out — to the transaction.

Every charge, checked

Each foreign transaction is measured against the European Central Bank's real daily rate — the rate banks give each other, not the one they give you.

Know your best card

Skimmed ranks your own cards by what they truly cost overseas, so you stop reaching for the expensive one.

Catch the DCC trap

"Pay in your home currency?" adds 4–8% at the terminal. Skimmed shows you exactly when to refuse.

A map of the leak

See which countries quietly cost you the most, grouped trip by trip — with a playbook for the next one.

How it works

Three steps. No spreadsheet.

1

Import a statement

CSV or PDF from any bank. No login, no linking, no account — just the file you already have.

2

See your number

Every foreign charge checked against the ECB's real daily rate, in seconds. The total your bank never showed you.

3

Fix your next trip

Know your best card, refuse the "pay in your home currency?" trap, and stop the leak for good.

Private by architecture

Nothing leaves your phone.

Skimmed was built so it can't spill your data — because it never has it.

Analysed on-device. Your statement is read on your iPhone and the raw file is never stored.

Delete anytime. Everything lives on your device and goes when you say so.
