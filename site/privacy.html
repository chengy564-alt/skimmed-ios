Privacy Policy

Last updated 15 July 2026

Skimmed ("we", "us", "the app") helps you understand what your cards actually cost you abroad. Because Skimmed handles financial information, protecting your data is central to how it is built. This policy explains, plainly, what Skimmed does and does not do with your data.

The short version

  • Skimmed analyses your bank or card statements entirely on your device.
  • Your statements, transactions, and card details never leave your iPhone and are never sent to us or anyone else.
  • There is no account, no login, and no connection to your bank.
  • We do not track you, show ads, or use third-party analytics.

Statements and financial data

When you import a statement (CSV or PDF), Skimmed reads and analyses it locally on your device.

  • Your raw statement file is never stored — it is discarded after analysis.
  • The parsed results (transactions, amounts, merchant names, a card nickname, and the last four digits of a card where available) are stored only on your device.
  • We never receive, upload, or have access to this information.
  • Full card numbers and bank login credentials are never requested, entered, or stored.

Exchange rates

To measure foreign charges against a fair rate, Skimmed requests published daily reference exchange rates from the European Central Bank's public data service. These requests contain only currency codes and dates. They never include your transactions, amounts, statements, or any personal or financial information.

Purchases

If you buy Skimmed Plus or a one-time report, the purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store. Skimmed does not receive or store your payment details. Apple's handling of your purchase is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Automatic-connection waitlist

Skimmed offers an optional waitlist for a future automatic bank-connection feature. If you choose to join, the details you enter (email, country, and bank) are stored on your device. In this version of the app, this information is not transmitted to us or any third party. If we later activate a waitlist service, we will use the email you provide solely to contact you about availability, and we will update this policy and the App Store privacy details before doing so.

Your photo

If you set a profile photo, it is stored only on your device.

Data retention and deletion

Everything Skimmed stores lives on your device. You can delete all imported data at any time from Settings, and deleting the app removes everything it stored.

Children

Skimmed is not directed at children. It is rated 4+ but intended for adults managing their own finances.

Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected here with a new "last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Message us at @Skimmedapps on X.
